History

Eminent Rafting Co. started with a simple idea: share the river with everyone, safely. From our base in Weija-Accra, we have guided families, friends and teams through their first rapids and their hundredth.

Today, we continue to guide visitors through exciting rivers while promoting teamwork, exploration and safety.
